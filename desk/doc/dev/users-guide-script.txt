## Natural Joins

## JOIN ON predicate

=uql "FROM adoptions A ".
"JOIN vaccinations V ".
"  ON A.name = V.name ".
" AND A.species = V.species ".
"SELECT A.name, A.species, A.adoption-date, V.vaccine, V.vaccination-time;"

=uql "FROM adoptions A ".
"CROSS JOIN vaccinations V ".
"WHERE A.name = V.name ".
"  AND A.species = V.species ".
"  AND V.vaccination-time > A.adoption-date ".
"SELECT A.name, A.species, A.adoption-date, V.vaccine, V.vaccination-time;"

## Outer Joins
